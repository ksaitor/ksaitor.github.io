I'm excited to share that [CryptoJobsList](https://cryptojobslist.com) has acquired [Crypto.jobs](https://crypto.jobs). Two job boards that started in 2017 are now coming together.

Back then, crypto was much smaller, and finding a job in the industry was tough. Over the years, Crypto.jobs helped more than 3,000 teams hire, including Consensys, CoinList and Parity. At CryptoJobsList, we've worked with over 4,000 projects and reached hundreds of thousands of job seekers each month.

Starting today, crypto.jobs redirects to CryptoJobsList. We're bringing the listings, employers and candidates together, and Crypto.jobs customers are moving over without a break in their hiring.

## Why this matters

Crypto hiring has outgrown the scattered job boards of the early days. Teams shouldn't have to manage split hiring pipelines, and candidates shouldn't have to wonder whether a job post is real.

There are plenty of job boards scraping listings without checking them. We review every job and company listing before it goes live. Employers also have access to verification and candidate screening tools, along with job posts, candidate search and applicant tracking in one place.

I'm grateful to the founder of Crypto.jobs for years of friendly competition and for making the handoff so smooth. Welcome to the CryptoJobsList community!

You can read more in the [CryptoJobsList acquisition announcement](https://cryptojobslist.com/blog/cryptojobslist-acquires-crypto-jobs).
